# CareerNet pilot corpus preparation

This research-only workflow prepares candidates for an English-language pilot on education, career expectations, and future uncertainty. The accepted [Phase 3D protocol](../phase_3d/README.md) supplies the ranking, within-asker representative selection, calendar strata, and broad relevance screen. Phase 3A supplies the separation between preparation and approved human or model access.

## Authorization and rights

The project relies on the advisor's existing written guidance for secondary analysis of publicly released, appropriately anonymous CC BY 4.0 data in this undergraduate project. No formal ethics committee determination has been issued. The existing guidance addresses the general ethics question. A repeat advisor reply or a new committee application is not a routine step for the pilot.

Before accessing the actual questions, record a source-specific data-processing assessment and obtain separate explicit owner authorization for the bounded run. The first run is limited to local, in-memory screening and sampling feasibility. The input may contain stable asker identifiers, identifying disclosures by minors, or health information. If relying on [Law No. 6698, Article 28(1)(c)](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/6649/Personal-Data-Protection-Law), document how the initial retrieval and inspection meet the provision's privacy and personal-rights conditions. Assess an applicable processing ground or an appropriately anonymous release if the scientific-purpose conditions cannot be justified. Automatic screening starts after access and can miss identifiable wording. The workflow never sends participant text to external inference services.

The [CareerNet v1.1 release](https://github.com/RenaissancePhilanthropy/careernet-data/releases/tag/v1.1) identifies `general_public_v1.1.csv` as CC BY 4.0. For later permitted sharing, preserve creator and source credit, link the [license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), and indicate filtering, normalization, screening, and sampling. The license covers copyright permissions within its scope. Privacy and other personality rights require their own assessment. This phase authorizes no sharing of individual questions or membership.

## Input and selection

The Windows CLI reads only the official general-domain release asset into bounded memory. Its exact size is 17,511,450 bytes and its pinned SHA-256 is `4e1583b68348ef741ca4abd8e52dec323c6cfc0bf761b15047c308370afb3017`. It verifies both before decoding, checks the full header and CSV structure, and projects only `question_id`, `asker_author_id`, `question_title`, `question_body`, and `question_added_at`. Answer, profile, location, and publisher label columns are discarded during parsing. Source asker identifiers stay in a separate parser lookup and are replaced with temporary group numbers before the question records are built. The lookup is discarded after parsing. Source question identifiers stay in memory for deduplication and stable ranking. Consistent repeated question rows collapse. Conflicting variants are screened for privacy and the whole question ID is excluded from sampling. The preparation excludes missing asker identifiers, fewer than 30 words, weak English signals, privacy flags, and exact or near duplicates. The English heuristic uses common function words and can misclassify multilingual or domain-specific text. Near-duplicate screening uses case-folded word-set Jaccard similarity of at least 0.90 among otherwise eligible questions of at least ten distinct words, excluding both sides of a match.

The fixed seed is 42. Cohort A takes up to 200 questions, at most one per asker group, with source-calendar strata and a separate stratum for missing or ambiguous dates. Naive times stay timezone-naive. The aggregate records the stratum representative frame size `N` and draw size `n`. The within-asker frame size `m` is computed in memory. Under the protocol's randomized-rank interpretation, the representative probability is `1/m` and the second-stage probability, conditional on the realized representative and its stratum, is `n/N`. The realized conditional product does not establish an unconditional inclusion probability when questions of one asker span strata. The deterministic realized sample and unweighted proportions cannot justify population-prevalence estimates.

Cohort B takes up to 200 provisional candidates, at most one per remaining asker identifier. The broad Phase 3D screen requires both a domain cue and an uncertainty cue and excludes all Cohort A question IDs and asker groups. Its results need contextual review and must be reported separately. General advice-seeking can trigger the screen. It establishes no anxiety or other semantic label. Shortfalls are reported as observed.

## Privacy screen and storage

Local patterns check contact details, direct identity cues, schools, locations, named workplaces, volunteered address or birth-date cues, possible minor-related disclosures and sensitive experiences. They can miss context and indirect or combined identifiers, and they can flag benign text. Whitespace and recognized markup are normalized without removing negation or rare words from the candidate text. Every flagged question is excluded before sampling. Where a flagged question is linked to a temporary asker group, all other questions in that group are excluded to limit cross-question linkage. If variants of one question conflict, collect all of their privacy categories and exclude all groups attached to that question when any variant is flagged. Each question contributes at most once per category. The aggregate counts distinct flagged question IDs, all privacy-excluded question IDs including linked questions, and excluded asker groups.

A high-risk combination means a credential cue, a possible minor with contact details, or a possible minor with both a name and school cue. A single such question triggers record and group exclusion. The run stops before sampling if the number of high-risk questions or the number of sensitive-experience flags reaches both 10 questions and 5% of distinct source question IDs, or if privacy screening finds flags and leaves no eligible questions. Integrity, input validation and storage checks also fail closed. A privacy stop retains source provenance and aggregate risk counts only. Console output consists of aggregates and fixed error codes. Small positive cells in privacy categories and year counts are grouped as 1–4 in console output. Review disclosure risk before sharing real aggregates, including sparse cells that could be inferred from other totals.

For an authorized run, prepare a new empty `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Hissiyat\phase-4c-<32 lowercase hex>` directory with access inheritance disabled. Set the current user's SID as owner and grant only that SID inheritable Full Control over files and subdirectories. Verify that the location is outside Git and excludes synced, shared and backed-up storage. The CLI checks for a canonical path, a fixed local drive and the expected ACL before requesting data and again before writing. Local administrators and software running as that user may still have access. Run from the repository root after completing the authorization and data-processing preflight:

```powershell
python -B -m research.phase_4c.prepare --approved-careernet-v1.1 "$privatePhase4cDirectory"
```

The argument acknowledges a separately documented decision. The script cannot verify the source-specific decision or owner authorization. The first run writes only `provenance.json` and `aggregate.json`. The raw CSV, question text, source identifiers, candidate memberships and row-level review flags remain off disk. No record is automatically human-review eligible or NLP-ready. A separately authorized human privacy review would need to reproduce the candidate selection from the pinned source, unchanged algorithm and seed, then assess which material is necessary to retain. The current CLI provides neither a review interface nor a text export. Review sparse cells, including counts inferable from other totals, before sharing aggregates.

The default retention is at most 30 days. For the undergraduate pilot, a documented exception may keep the source provenance and necessary aggregate statistics through 28 February 2027, Istanbul time, to reproduce the reported figures around the expected mid-January presentation and address thesis corrections. Treat this as a ceiling and use an earlier date when it meets the research need. Record the reason in the private preflight before using the optional `--retain-until YYYY-MM-DD` argument. An explicit date after the ceiling and any elapsed deadline stop the run before retrieval. A privacy stop keeps its non-identifying summary on the shorter deadline. The longer period applies only to the two aggregate-only artifacts. Future participant-level outputs require their own scope and retention decision.

Provenance records the cutoff in UTC. The workspace owner manually deletes the run artifacts as soon as they are no longer needed, the research is completed, authorization is withdrawn, or the deadline is reached, whichever comes first, and verifies removal. If the script reports `private_output_cleanup_failed` or is forcibly interrupted during a write, inspect the restricted run directory and remove any partial outputs before retrying. The script does not delete files automatically.
