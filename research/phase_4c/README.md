# CareerNet pilot corpus preparation

This research-only workflow prepares candidates for an English-language pilot on education, career expectations, and future uncertainty. The accepted [Phase 3D protocol](../phase_3d/README.md) supplies the ranking, within-asker representative selection, calendar strata, and broad relevance screen. Phase 3A supplies the separation between preparation and approved human or model access.

## Authorization and rights

The project relies on the advisor's existing written guidance for secondary analysis of publicly released, appropriately anonymous CC BY 4.0 data in this undergraduate project. No formal ethics committee determination has been issued. The existing guidance addresses the general ethics question. A repeat advisor reply or a new committee application is not a routine step for the pilot.

Before accessing the actual questions, obtain separate explicit owner authorization for the bounded run and record the source-specific data-processing basis. The intended use is local, minimized screening and corpus preparation for scientific research. The source may contain stable asker identifiers, identifying disclosures by minors, or health information before screening. If relying on the scientific-purpose provision of [Law No. 6698, Article 28(1)(c)](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/6649/Personal-Data-Protection-Law), document how the actual handling meets its privacy and personal-rights conditions. Where those conditions cannot be justified, identify an applicable processing ground, including for special-category data where relevant, or use an appropriately anonymous release. Automatic screening takes place after access, and pseudonymous aliases leave the question wording potentially identifying. The workflow never sends participant text to external inference services.

The [CareerNet v1.1 release](https://github.com/RenaissancePhilanthropy/careernet-data/releases/tag/v1.1) identifies `general_public_v1.1.csv` as CC BY 4.0. For later permitted sharing, preserve creator and source credit, link the [license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), and indicate filtering, normalization, screening, and sampling. The license covers copyright permissions within its scope. Privacy and other personality rights require their own assessment. This phase authorizes no sharing of individual questions or membership.

## Input and selection

The Windows CLI streams only the official general-domain release asset into bounded memory. Its exact size is 17,511,450 bytes and its pinned SHA-256 is `4e1583b68348ef741ca4abd8e52dec323c6cfc0bf761b15047c308370afb3017`. It verifies both before decoding, checks the full header and CSV structure, and projects only `question_id`, `asker_author_id`, `question_title`, `question_body`, and `question_added_at`. Answer, profile, location, and publisher label columns are never carried into the research records. Consistent repeated question rows collapse. Conflicting variants are screened for privacy and the whole question ID is excluded from sampling. The preparation excludes missing asker identifiers, fewer than 30 words, weak English signals, privacy flags, and exact or near duplicates. The English heuristic uses common function words and can misclassify multilingual or domain-specific text. Near-duplicate screening uses case-folded word-set Jaccard similarity of at least 0.90 among otherwise eligible questions of at least ten distinct words, excluding both sides of a match.

The fixed seed is 42. Cohort A takes up to 200 questions, at most one per asker identifier, with source-calendar strata and a separate stratum for missing or ambiguous dates. Naive times stay timezone-naive. The design records the within-asker frame size `m` per selection and the stratum representative frame size `N` and draw size `n`. Under the protocol's randomized-rank interpretation, the representative probability is `1/m` and the second-stage probability, conditional on the realized representative and its stratum, is `n/N`. The realized conditional product does not establish an unconditional inclusion probability when questions of one asker span strata. The deterministic realized sample and unweighted proportions cannot justify population-prevalence estimates.

Cohort B takes up to 200 provisional candidates, at most one per remaining asker identifier. The broad Phase 3D screen requires both a domain cue and an uncertainty cue and excludes all Cohort A question IDs and asker groups. Its results need contextual review and must be reported separately. General advice-seeking can trigger the screen. It establishes no anxiety or other semantic label. Shortfalls are reported as observed.

## Privacy screen and storage

Local patterns check obvious contact details, direct identity cues, schools, locations, possible minor-related disclosures and sensitive experiences. They can miss context and indirect or combined identifiers, and they can flag benign text. Whitespace and recognized markup are normalized without removing negation or rare words from the candidate text. Flagged records are excluded from selection. A credential cue, a possible minor combined with contact details, a possible minor combined with both a name and school cue, or sensitive-experience cues affecting at least 5% of distinct question IDs and at least 10 questions stops the run before sampling. Conflicting variants contribute at most once per risk category for each question ID. Only source provenance and aggregate risk counts are retained on this path. Console output consists of aggregates and fixed error codes. Small positive cells in privacy categories and year counts are grouped as 1–4 in console output. Review disclosure risk before sharing real aggregates, including sparse cells that could be inferred from other totals.

For an authorized run, prepare a new empty `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Hissiyat\phase-4c-<32 lowercase hex>` directory with access inheritance disabled. Set the current user's SID as owner and grant only that SID inheritable Full Control over files and subdirectories. Verify that the location is outside Git and excludes synced, shared and backed-up storage. The CLI checks for a canonical path, a fixed local drive and the expected ACL before requesting data and again before writing. Local administrators and software running as that user may still have access. Run from the repository root after completing the authorization and data-processing preflight:

```powershell
python -B -m research.phase_4c.prepare --approved-careernet-v1.1 "$privatePhase4cDirectory"
```

The argument acknowledges a separately documented decision. The script cannot verify the source-specific decision or owner authorization. It retains no raw CSV on disk. Each run produces source provenance and aggregate statistics, plus separate candidate text, membership, and flag-code files on a successful preparation. Aliases use a per-run ephemeral key, so the same source and seed reproduce selection but not the aliases. Membership is stored separately from text, carries no raw question or asker identifier, and explicitly requires human privacy review. The flag file retains codes and run-local aliases only. Flagged source text is unavailable from the output and any reinspection requires separate authorization. Review prepared records for indirect identification and disclosures involving minors before clearing any input for model inference. No record is automatically human-review eligible or NLP-ready.

Keep this run directory for at most 30 days, ending sooner if work is stopped or authorization is withdrawn. Provenance records the UTC deletion deadline. The workspace owner must remove only artifacts created by this run, verify removal, and review retention separately if a later protocol is approved. If the script reports `private_output_cleanup_failed` or is forcibly interrupted during a write, inspect the restricted run directory and remove any partial outputs before retrying. Automatic deletion and physical erasure guarantees are outside this workflow.
